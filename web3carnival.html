What is Web3 Carnival?

Embrace the spectacle of tomorrow

The Web3 Carnival is the world's premier Blockchain & Crypto event, exploring every facet of Web3 innovation guided by industry leaders, investors and experts, across Blockchain, DAOs, the Metaverse, DeFi, ZK, GameFi, Enterprise and NFTs, with daily side events and a cultural tapestry of networking woven through every edition.

Empowering Women, Accelerating Startups Web3 Carnival fuses a DAO-like mission of empowering women and fuelling startups. Think of it as your mainnet launch: a canvas where groundbreaking ideas fork and disrupt, with women and startups as the nodes that power the ecosystem.
Vision & Mission

Where the carnival is headed

Web3 Carnival envisions a dynamic blend of innovation and empowerment, knitting together a vibrant and inclusive Web3 community. Our mission is to pioneer the future of the Web3 landscape by uniting industry leaders, pushing the limits of the Web3 arena, fostering inclusivity and accessibility for young enthusiasts, and elevating this ecosystem to new heights.

Why partner with the carnival

A mega tech event across the globe, and amidst all possibilities.

  1. Exponential reachJoin 5,000+ attendees and reach an engaged audience that carries your story across the blockchain arena.
  2. Undeniable credibilityAlign with top-tier industry leaders and become a trusted source of reach, insight and cutting-edge information.
  3. Exclusive coverageInterviews, behind-the-scenes access and content built to create a buzz online.

    Legal

    Terms and privacy, in plain words

    A readable summary for this redesign concept. The organiser's full legal text governs any real registration.

    Terms of Service

    Applies to registrations, applications and attendance.

    1. Registration. Submitting the form requests a seat or application review. A place is confirmed only by email from the team.
    2. Tickets. Passes are personal and non-transferable unless the organisers agree in writing.
    3. Programme changes. Speakers, agenda and venues can change. Updates go to the email you registered with.
    4. Conduct. Attendees follow the event code of conduct. The organisers may refuse or withdraw entry for breaches.
    5. Media. Photography and filming take place on site, and attendees may appear in event coverage.
    6. Cancellation and refund. Refund eligibility and deadlines are shown at checkout on the ticketing platform. Requests go to hello@web3carnival.world.

    Privacy Policy

    Covers the details you share through this page.

    1. What we collect. Name, email, organisation and application type from the registration form, and your email if you subscribe.
    2. Why. To process registrations and applications, and to send event updates you asked for.
    3. Sharing. Your details are never sold. They go to sponsors or partners only with your explicit consent.
    4. Retention. Records are kept for as long as needed to run the event and meet legal obligations.
    5. Your rights. Write to hello@web3carnival.world to access, correct or delete your data, or to unsubscribe.
